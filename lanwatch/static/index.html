What do trust levels mean?

Trust only changes how lanwatch reports changes. It never blocks a device.

Unknown

Seen on the network but not reviewed yet. New devices start here.

Trusted

Gear you recognize. If it goes offline, the change is highlighted as a warning.

Ignored

Kept in the inventory, but left out of change events and alerts.

Change trust in any device's details. Everything stays on this device.
